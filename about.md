## InjuryIQ
AFL injury intelligence — depth over headlines. InjuryIQ is a source-cited record of every AFL injury, every club and every round, classified to OSIICS medical standards. It pairs that record with squad-health comparisons across all 18 clubs, round-by-round matchup health, player durability profiles and in-depth analysis of what the injury news actually means.

* [InjuryIQ](https://injuryiq.vercel.app)
* [InjuryIQ on Substack](https://injuryiq.substack.com)

## Power Rankings
ELO-based power rankings for basketball competitions. Raw game results are turned into margin-adjusted, strength-of-schedule weighted ratings, with a ranking snapshot preserved every round so team trajectories can be tracked across a season. Coverage includes school competitions such as AIC, GPS and TAS, CBSQ tournaments, the U18 SQJBC Premier League and the NBL.

* [Power Rankings](https://power-rankings-zeta.vercel.app)

## Demonwiki
Established in 2009, Demonwiki draws on sources including newspapers, books, websites, interviews and personal memories to chronicle the history of the world's oldest professional sporting club. There are currently over 8350 articles and 6200 images in our database.

* [Demonwiki](http://demonwiki.org)
